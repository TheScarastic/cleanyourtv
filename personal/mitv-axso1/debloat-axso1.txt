# - PatchWall was kept at first, then disabled in batch 5 once the new launcher was in use (see there).
# - Kept by choice: Google Assistant, Chromecast, Mi Cast/Miracast, SonyLIV, ZEE5, Hungama, JioCinema,
#   Spotify, Kodi, YouTube Music, Play Games, Mi Music, Mi Gallery, Sensara TV guide.
# - com.xiaomi.mitv.updateservice is disabled by choice. It is NOT the firmware updater: decompiled, it only
#   updates PatchWall and Xiaomi apps from in.tv.global.mi.com, and with individual_privacy_enabled=0 it never
#   contacts the server (it answers itself with a local 404). Firmware OTAs come through Google Play Services
#   (com.google.android.gms/.update.SystemUpdatePanoActivity), which stays enabled. Re-enable it if you
#   accept Xiaomi's privacy policy and want PatchWall updates.
# - com.google.android.tvrecommendations also feeds Google sign-in notices: if Play Store ever says
#   "Authentication is required", pm enable it (see examples/xiaomi-mitv-axso2/debloat.txt).
# - The stock launcher com.google.android.tvlauncher is disabled by launcher/build-and-install.ps1 once the
#   new launcher is Home, not by this list.
#

# Undo:   $env:TV_ADB_BINARY='adb'; tools\restore.ps1 -TvHost <tv ip>:5555 personal\mitv-axso1\debloat-axso1.txt
